This application is an official academic support platform developed to assist students of Abdulkadir Kure University, Minna.

It provides students with quick and reliable access to essential academic and campus information, including:

📅 Lecture and examination timetables with smart reminders

🧾 Academic results, grading system, CGPA, and degree classifications

🏫 Faculties, departments, programmes, and school structure

📰 University news and announcements

🤖 An intelligent AI assistant for school-related and academic guidance

The AI assistant is designed to:

Respond accurately using official school data

Clearly indicate when information is not from the school database

Provide general academic guidance when official data is unavailable

Work offline for school-related information

Use external AI services only when necessary and when quota is available
