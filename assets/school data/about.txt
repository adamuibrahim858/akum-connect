About Abdulkadir Kure University, Minna

Abdulkadir Kure University, Minna (AKUM) is a public, state-owned university located in Minna, Niger State, Nigeria. The university was established in 2023 by the Niger State Government and is named after Engr. Abdulkadir Kure, a former Governor of Niger State (1999–2007).

Originally founded as a University of Education, AKUM was later converted into a full-fledged conventional university to expand academic offerings and meet the growing demand for higher education in the state and beyond.

The university is recognized for its focus on knowledge, innovation, sustainability, and green development, making it one of the first institutions in Nigeria to integrate environmental sustainability and low-carbon development strategies into its academic mission.

Key Highlights:

Type: Public (Niger State Government–owned)

Established: 2023

Location: Minna, Niger State, Nigeria

Motto: Knowledge and Innovation for Sustainable Development

Vice-Chancellor: Prof. Mohammed Aliyu Paiko

Pro-Chancellor: Prof. Mohammed Kuta Yahaya

Academic Focus: Education, Science, Arts & Social Sciences, Technical and Vocational Studies, with expanding programmes in Medicine, ICT, Environmental Sciences, and Renewable Energy

Special Strength: Green economy, sustainability, innovation, and entrepreneurship

Recent Initiatives: Free tuition for pioneer students, reduced registration fees, major investment in hostels, senate building, and research facilities

AKUM is one of the fastest-growing public universities in Nigeria and is positioned as a model institution for sustainable development, innovation-driven learning, and community impact in Niger State and Nigeria at large.
